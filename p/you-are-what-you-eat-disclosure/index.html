You Are What You Eat; Disclosure

My last article.

7 minute read

Something important may be learned from the systems thinking heuristic, The Purpose of a System is What it Does. The tendency to understand human beings and their historical development through professed intentions rather than through the actual activity is challenged. Considering man frequently understands himself through what he intends to accomplish and what he believes himself to be doing, or what he considers his purpose to be, his actual activity may reveal something quite different. There is a distinction between the appearance of an action and what this activity actually accomplishes. Such a distinction surely shouldn’t be understood as the suggestion that intentions are irrelevant or that we walk around stumbling, unconscious and incapable of consciously determining our actions. Rather, intentions alone do not establish the objective significance of an activity. Consider an individual who sets out to establish this or that institution to promote equality. Its stated purpose is equality, and its members may sincerely believe themselves to be pursuing those ends. However, if its actual operations continuously produces hierarchical dependencies and inequalities, then the wise decision is to then investigate the institution in terms of what it actually does.

We may extend this to the activity of man. To what extent is man conscious of the objective significance of his own activity? Acting within a meshed network of relationships and inherited conditions, his activity enters into this objective context - the world - reproducing and transforming the conditions that subsequently confront him as an objective reality. Man is thus the product of some existing developmental history, and a participant exhibiting an activity in relation to this developmental world.

The phrase “you are what you eat” demonstrates this principle quite well. Man not only is what he intends to be, but what he continuously makes of himself through the organic process. Any organism is inseparable from what it continually assimilates and the substance it resides in. Its actual constitution is entirely determined by what it does and what it incorporates into self. Shaped by what he consumes, practices, experiences, and reproduces, his intellectual, cultural, social, and physical constitution is occasioned by the content with which he engages and which he assimilates. Ergo, man is what he makes of himself through what he takes in and reproduces.

Now, there is a broader problem implied here of development; how something comes into being and how its previous conditions remain operative or are transformed, and how the whole of this idea is transformed through an ensuing development. There is a relationship between the successive stages of existence. An organism, for instance, develops through some succession, with each stage contributing to the constitution of the whole organism. That is to say, any given immediacy of presentation is the result of some process, one by which the past characteristics and formative principles of an earlier stage, when developed, proceed into a subsequent one. Some characteristics disappear as distinct forms, others persist, and still others reappear in modified configurations. The whole schema mirrors the parable of the sower. Regardless, a proposition traditionally associated with biologist Ernst Haeckel, concerns the relationship between the development of an individual, and the development of a species. The genesis of each scope is therefore named ontogeny and phylogeny, concluding in the proposition: ontogeny recapitulates phylogeny, claiming that the development of an individual reproduces aspects of the evolutionary history of its species. As relevant to us, the significance of recapitulation extends past mineralistic, biological development. The principle applies to the development of the Earth and humanity, to the whole of the great chain of being.

Recapitulation describes the reappearance of an earlier developmental stage within a subsequent stage. Of consideration here is that the earlier stage appears within a developmental context. Characteristics are incorporated into a new configuration and acquire a different significance through their relationships with what has subsequently developed. Development always involves a polarity of continuity and differentiation wherein earlier formative principles exist, while their expression changes as the organism or evolutionary process advances. Remembrance concerns the relationship between successive evolutionary periods within a stage, or how an earlier evolutionary impulse reappears within a later period. Later periods of human development carry something of earlier periods within themselves. For instance, an earlier cultural or spiritual impulse can reappear in a subsequent historical period through an entirely different expression.

Practical examples may be of use. Egyptian civilization recapitulates the broader stage of Lemurian/Pyrean/Thermean civilization. During Lemuria (as shorthand), man acquired certain characteristics such as upright posture. In Egyptian civilization, these earlier developments were brought to light in a more conscious way through the expression of Egyptian architecture. Obelisks express this principle of verticality, and the Sphinx prepared man to understand the significance of speech which also began in this period. The Pyramids also express the significance of man’s uprightness. The remembrance of Egyptian knowledge, that is, knowledge concerning the cosmos, was carried forth through reincarnation by individuals who had experienced it and carried certain impressions and impulses into subsequent incarnations. Kepler, Copernicus, and Paracelsus stand as examples of the remembrance of earlier Egyptian knowledge.

All this to say that, when examining an evolutionary period, one should investigate what it actually produces, inducing how precisely it incorporates inherited developmental conditions. So what of this information? This is exactly the point- what do we do with information and in what fashion? How does one determine the truth if it is always in situ? Heidegger becomes the main critic here in the modern day treatment of correspondence. Correspondence between thought and being, between proposition and reality, is problematized by Heidegger’s concept of disclosure. Because we are always in some form of processual state, the only thing we can do is uncover and bring forth what we can authentically, within the particular horizon of intelligibility we inhabit. Disclosure is historically situated and relative to the conditions through which something becomes intelligible, which is always being produced. Surely, if development consisted exclusively of repeating previously established conditions without introducing anything novel, the universe would not move. Only the abstract repetition of what was already there is an impossibility, and so truth, like development, must be considered in relation to the conditions through which something becomes manifest. The condition in question is man.

The significance of spiritual science, anthroposophy in specific, is that here it addresses precisely this relationship between the development of human being and the disclosure of reality. Steiner’s conception is premised on the possibility that human cognition itself has and is currently undergoing development, thereby transforming the conditions through which reality becomes intelligible. Man is consequently, the agent through which the content of the universe becomes accessible to consciousness.

Without actually doing this activity, all knowledge to be had is negative- it is what Schelling calls a negative metaphysics, as opposed to a “metaphysical empiricism” which would be the productive activity befitting of man. Even Hegel’s formal knowledge is a form which is a content-form that overdetermines the real being of a thing. We cannot advance our understanding of reality without actively developing the cognitive capacities through which it becomes accessible. The advanced initiates during the Lemurian age, where man was animalistic, had the capabilities of memory. The advanced initiates during Atlantean times, the times where memory and water ruled, could think. And now during the Aryan epoch, the advanced initiates have clairvoyance. The same dynamics rhyme, except now, the distinction between seers and initiates is mandated to become dissolved.

It is because of this fact that this blog will be discontinued. Effort on my part needs to be put in towards other avenues, namely YouTube. Over the year this blog has been up, it has helped tremendously in my own path. It has helped me articulate things I have never considered doing so, and has helped give me direction towards the productive projects I want to do in the future. Because I have done this activity, because I have developed my inner voice and personality through writing, it has similarly improved my own capacity for reading the world; the book of nature. The dictum truly stands undefeated, you are what you eat. What will you consume and integrate into yourself, and what will you do after?

-Iapetus Janus Domain
